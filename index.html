🏛️ Two-Stage Deep Learning Architecture

1. Stage B (Self-Supervised ViT-MAE): 75% patch masking on multi-channel satellite surface inputs with 2D sinusoidal geospatial embeddings.

2. Stage C (Cross-Attention Depth Decoder): 15 learned depth query tokens decode surface latents into 15 discrete levels from 0 to 1000 meters.

⚖️ Differentiable Gravitational Stability Loss

$$\mathcal{L}_{stab} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{z} \max\left(0, T(z_{k+1}) - T(z_k) - \epsilon\right)^2$$

Penalizes unstable density inversions at training time, mathematically ensuring 100% monotonic temperature profiles in the open ocean.
